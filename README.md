# Moiz Rana

I sell for a living. Doha based, ten years of B2B and government work across the Gulf. In June 2025 I started teaching myself to build with AI, no CS background, and shipped 30 apps in 30 days to learn. Most of what's here came out of that run, and I kept going after it ended.

I built everything here end to end, with Claude as my pair.

## Highlights

| App | What it is |
|---|---|
| [likhai](https://github.com/moizbuilds/likhai) | Urdu writing app in Nastaliq script. Live at [likhai-gamma.vercel.app](https://likhai-gamma.vercel.app). Benchmarked Claude at 96% accuracy vs a 68% baseline on a gold test set before shipping. |
| [qatar-dental-prep](https://github.com/moizbuilds/qatar-dental-prep) | RAG study platform for Qatar's MOPH/DHP dental licensing exam. Every answer cites its source from 32 official reference textbooks. |
| [fleetiq](https://github.com/moizbuilds/fleetiq) | Fleet maintenance SaaS. Next.js, Postgres, Clerk, Claude. 186 automated tests. |
| [wasfaiq](https://github.com/moizbuilds/wasfaiq) | Adapts recipes into Gulf Arabic using JAIS (G42's Arabic LLM) with an automatic Claude fallback. |
| [worldcup-scout](https://github.com/moizbuilds/worldcup-scout) | Multi-agent football scouting dashboard. Specialised agents evaluate and rank players. |
| [vibe-and-thread](https://github.com/moizbuilds/vibe-and-thread) | AI product matching across 28 Qatar retail stores, fed by a daily scraping pipeline on Cloud Functions. |
| [cortex](https://github.com/moizbuilds/cortex) | AI corporate training platform with its own eval suite. |

## How I work

Sales taught me to find the real problem before building anything. The apps taught me the rest: RAG pipelines, multi-agent orchestration, evals before quality claims, keys server-side, rate limits on anything that costs money. I write TypeScript and Python, deploy on Vercel, and store data in Postgres more often than not.
